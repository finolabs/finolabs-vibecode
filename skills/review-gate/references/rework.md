# Rework rounds

Detail behind `SKILL.md` "Findings". Read it when a review round ends with blocking findings; the enforcer named there applies here unchanged.

Rework rounds: at most the recorded rework limit (default two; `Rework limit` under `## Reviews` of the host config, set in `setup`).

After the last, stop and give the owner each remaining blocking finding with its evidence, to fix further, accept as risk or change direction.

During a live `afk` lease, this hand-off goes through `afk` "Decide" instead of waiting for the owner.
